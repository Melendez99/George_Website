A portfolio build for TikTok Shop and affiliate sellers. A product photo is analysed, an AI writes the image and scene prompts, a model image is generated and Veo creates the video clips, which are then merged into one ad and saved with a link in Airtable. A second version runs from a Google Sheet on a schedule.

## What the system does

- Reads the product photo and description and writes the prompts
- Generates a starting frame and the video scenes, waiting and polling until each is ready
- Merges the clips into one video and stores the link
- Updates the sheet or Airtable row when it is done
