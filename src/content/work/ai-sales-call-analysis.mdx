Kristin Eifler wanted consistent feedback on sales calls without a manager listening to every recording. I built a Make.com scenario that takes the call transcript, runs it through ten scoring sections based on her own scoring documents, and writes structured reports for sales leaders and advisers.

## What the system does

- Picks up new call transcripts automatically
- Scores each call against ten sections using Claude
- Produces separate reports for the sales leader and the adviser
