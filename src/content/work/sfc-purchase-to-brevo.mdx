Coy Swaim runs Simple Freakin' Care and wanted new members to get a welcome email automatically after they buy. The purchase webhook did not include the buyer's name or email, so I built a match between the order and the lead record, and tested it with a live purchase before launch.

## What the system does

- Checks the webhook secret so only genuine purchase events get through
- Finds the right sub-account and matches the order to the buyer's lead
- Checks email consent before anything is sent, with alerts for no match or missing consent
- Creates or updates the contact in Brevo, adds it to the members list and sends the welcome email
- Feeds members into a weekly email series built in Brevo

## Scope note

Cancellation emails were left out at the client's request, so Brevo's unsubscribe link handles members who leave.
