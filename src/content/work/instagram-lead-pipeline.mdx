A sales team wanted a steady flow of people into its webinar without spending hours prospecting on Instagram. The key finding was to scrape the commenters on popular posts, who are engaged, rather than random followers, whose profiles are often empty.

## What the system does

- Runs on a schedule and pulls recent posts from chosen accounts
- Scrapes commenters on the posts that get real engagement
- Scores each profile with Claude and drops the cold ones
- Finds a public email, verifies uncertain ones with Hunter.io, and saves everything to Airtable
- Writes a personalised email for each lead and sends it through Instantly, split into a business track and a membership track
