Daniel Grozev wanted to turn long videos into short clips and post them across platforms without doing it by hand. I built a set of n8n workflows that send each uploaded video to Vizard for clipping, save every clip to Notion for review, and schedule the approved ones through Ayrshare. The pipeline later gained YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and LinkedIn, and I maintain it as a retainer.

## What the system does

- Picks up new videos from a Google Drive folder and sends them for clipping automatically
- Saves each clip to Notion with its title, viral score and transcript so it can be reviewed and approved
- Pairs approved scripts with approved clips and schedules them across platforms, spread over the month
- Marks failed projects so nothing disappears silently
- Copies clips to permanent storage, because clip links from the clipping tool expire after seven days
