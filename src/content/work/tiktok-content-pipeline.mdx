A creator wanted to turn long videos into short clips and post them across platforms without doing it by hand. I built an n8n pipeline that sends each uploaded video to Vizard for clipping, saves every clip to Notion for review, and schedules the approved ones through Ayrshare.

## What the system does

- Picks up new videos from a Google Drive folder and sends them for clipping automatically
- Saves each clip to Notion with its title, score and transcript so it can be reviewed and approved
- Schedules approved clips across platforms on a posting calendar
- Marks failed projects so nothing disappears silently
