Jerry Jennings was launching an agency for local service businesses and needed a front-desk system he could resell. I built an n8n workflow that texts the caller back as soon as a call is missed and hands the conversation to an AI agent that can book an appointment. Watch the walkthroughs above to see it run.

## What the system does

- Receives the inbound text through a Twilio webhook and looks up which business it belongs to in Airtable, so one workflow serves many clients
- Replies with an AI agent that keeps the conversation in Supabase memory
- Checks Google Calendar availability and creates the booking
- Suggests another time when the requested slot is taken
- Sends the reply back by SMS through Twilio
