An agency serving home-service businesses was losing leads when the phone went unanswered. I built an n8n system that texts the caller back straight away and hands them to an AI booking agent, so the lead can book without waiting for a callback. Watch the walkthroughs above to see it run.

## What the system does

- Detects missed calls and sends a text-back right away
- Lets an AI agent check Google Calendar availability and book the appointment
- Suggests another time when the requested slot is taken
- Logs every lead and outcome in Airtable so the business can see what came in
