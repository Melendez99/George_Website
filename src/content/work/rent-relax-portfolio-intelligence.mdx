Brenton Hitchcock runs Rent & Relax and wanted his Streamline data made useful: what changed, what is underperforming and how each property compares with its market. I built the data layer in n8n and Supabase and connected it to ChatGPT through an MCP connector, so he can ask questions about current and historical data in plain language.

## What the system does

- Pulls properties, availability, rates and reservations from Streamline every morning and keeps the history
- Checks for rate changes, owner holds and maintenance blocks, and booking pace drops, and stores each finding
- Compares properties with a Key Data comp set and market KPIs
- Emails a daily digest that groups properties by the reason they were flagged
- Keeps weekly snapshots of market position so trends can be reviewed later
- Gives ChatGPT the data and the listing links it needs to answer questions about the portfolio
