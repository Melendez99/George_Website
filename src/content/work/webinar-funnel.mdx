A webinar business wanted registration, reminders and follow-up to run without manual work. I built six n8n workflows around GoHighLevel, Zoom and Twilio.

## What the system does

- Registers attendees from the funnel into the CRM
- Sends reminder sequences by email and SMS before the event
- Tracks attendance from Zoom and tags attendees and no-shows
- Runs follow-up sequences based on attendance
