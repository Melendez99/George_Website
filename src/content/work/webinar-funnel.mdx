Anthony Edwards ran webinars and wanted registration, reminders and follow-up to run without manual work. I built six n8n workflows around GoHighLevel, Zoom, Gmail and Twilio: registration and eligibility, reminder sequence, attendance tracking, post-webinar follow-up, no-show follow-up and replay tracking.

## What the system does

- Registers attendees from the funnel into the CRM
- Sends reminder sequences by email and SMS before the event
- Tracks attendance from Zoom and tags attendees and no-shows
- Runs follow-up sequences based on attendance, including a day 0, 1, 3 and 7 sequence for no-shows
