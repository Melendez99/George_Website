Brenton Hitchcock wanted a steady stream of local blog content for Hitchcock Properties without writing it all by hand. I built an n8n engine that runs three times a week, takes the next topic from a Google Sheet, and does the research, writing and publishing work.

## What the system does

- Pulls local market data and web research for the topic
- Plans and writes the post with Claude, then generates the SEO title and description
- Finds a cover image and uploads it to WordPress with the post as a draft, with Yoast fields filled in
- Creates matching social posts through Publer
- Emails a notice and marks the topic as published in the sheet so it never runs twice

## One honest limit

The client's second site runs on Sierra Interactive, whose support confirmed its API cannot create blog posts. Cross-posting there stays a manual copy and paste step.
