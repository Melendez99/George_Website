When a customer calls a service business and nobody answers, they rarely leave a voicemail. They call the next company on the list. A missed-call text-back system fixes this by sending a text within seconds of the missed call, so the conversation starts before the customer moves on.

## How missed-call text-back works

1. A call to your business number goes unanswered, or is busy.
2. Your phone system or Twilio sends an event to a webhook.
3. A workflow texts the caller: "Sorry we missed you. How can we help?"
4. The caller replies, and an AI agent or a person carries on the conversation.
5. The agent checks your calendar and books the appointment.

The first text is the valuable part. Once the caller replies, you are talking to someone who still wants the job done.

## The n8n and Twilio setup I use

This is one of my builds, shown unedited. It runs in n8n:

- **Inbound SMS webhook:** Twilio sends every incoming text here.
- **Lookup step:** the workflow finds which business the message belongs to in Airtable, so one workflow can serve many businesses.
- **AI agent:** it answers in the business's voice and keeps the conversation history in Supabase so it remembers earlier messages.
- **Calendar tools:** the agent checks Google Calendar availability and creates the booking. When a slot is taken, it suggests another time.
- **Twilio reply:** the answer goes back to the customer by SMS.

## What to decide before you build

- **What the first text says.** Keep it short and in your normal tone. Do not pretend to be a person.
- **What the agent may do.** Book, answer common questions, collect details. Anything else goes to a human.
- **Opening hours.** Decide whether the agent books outside working hours.
- **Consent and rules.** Texting rules depend on your country and your customer's. Check them before you go live, and include a way to stop messages.
- **Where the data goes.** Leads should land in your CRM or sheet, with a note to your team.

## Does it need an AI agent?

No. The simplest version only sends the first text with a booking link. That already recovers part of the leads you lose today. The AI agent is the next step, when you want the system to handle the back-and-forth and book for you.

## Common questions

**Can this work with my existing phone number?**
Usually yes. Calls can be forwarded to a Twilio number, or your phone provider can send missed-call events to a webhook. The right route depends on your provider.

**Is it better in n8n or Make?**
Both can do it. I pick n8n when the flow needs an AI agent with memory and tools. See [n8n vs Make](/blog/n8n-vs-make-which-should-you-pick) for how I decide.

**What does it cost to run?**
You pay for Twilio messages, your AI model usage and the tool hosting. These are usage-based, so the cost depends on how many calls you miss.
