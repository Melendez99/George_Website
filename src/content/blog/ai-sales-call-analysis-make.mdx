Sales managers cannot listen to every recorded call. Calls go unreviewed, and reps get feedback only on the few a manager has time to hear. AI call analysis scores every transcript the same way, so feedback arrives for every call.

## What AI sales call analysis does

You give it a transcript and a scoring framework. It reads the call and rates each part of the conversation, such as discovery questions, objection handling and the close. Then it writes a short report with what went well and what to fix.

## A real Make scenario

![Make scenario that takes call transcripts, scores them in ten sections with AI and creates reports](/images/blog/ai-sales-call-analysis-make/make-sales-call-analysis-scenario.webp)

This is a scenario I built in Make for a sales coaching client, shown unedited. It does this:

1. **Intake.** It picks up new call transcripts automatically.
2. **Scoring.** It runs each call through ten scoring sections, based on the client's own scoring documents, using Claude.
3. **Reports.** It writes separate reports for the sales leader and for the adviser who took the call.

The client's own framework drives the scoring, so the feedback sounds like their coaching and not a generic checklist. There is a walkthrough video in the [sales call analysis case study](/work/ai-sales-call-analysis).

## What you need

- Call transcripts from your recording tool (many tools export these)
- A written scoring framework: what a good call looks like, section by section
- A Make account and an AI model account
- A place to store reports, such as Google Drive

## Make the scoring useful

- **Write clear criteria.** "Asked about budget before presenting" is easier to score than "good discovery".
- **Test on calls you know.** Run calls your team already rated and compare.
- **Keep a human in the loop.** Use scores to guide coaching conversations, not to replace them.
- **Mind privacy.** Call recordings contain personal data. Check consent rules and where your AI provider processes data.

**Does it work for coaching, real estate or insurance calls?**

Yes. Reports can go to Drive, email, Slack or your CRM.
